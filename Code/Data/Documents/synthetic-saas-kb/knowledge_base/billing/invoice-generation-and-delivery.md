# Invoice Generation & Delivery

This article explains when invoices are generated for AcmeFlow Workspace, how they are delivered, and where to find them. Invoices serve as the billing statement for each subscription cycle and are generated automatically.

## When Invoices Are Generated

- Invoices are generated at each billing cycle close.
- Monthly invoices are generally available the day the charge posts; exact posting time can vary by a few hours.
- Annual invoices are generated at the annual renewal date.
- Invoices are also generated for prorated charges, seat changes, and other mid-cycle adjustments.

Every charge that posts to your payment method corresponds to an invoice you can review in your Workspace.

## How Invoices Are Delivered

Two things happen when an invoice is generated:

1. **A notification email is sent** to the workspace billing contact.
2. **The invoice is made available** in the Workspace Billing section.

The billing contact receives the notification but does not receive a PDF attachment by default. The full invoice, including line items, is always available in the Workspace.

## What an Invoice Contains

Each invoice shows line items that reflect:

- Subscription base charge for the cycle
- Prorations (for example, mid-cycle upgrades or added seats)
- Seat changes
- Taxes where applicable
- Credits applied where applicable

The invoice also shows the invoice number, the period covered, the billing address on file, the payment method used, and the total charged.

**Example:** A Pro workspace upgraded from 6 to 8 seats mid-cycle. Its next invoice lists the base monthly charge, a prorated line for the 2 added seats, applicable tax, and the final total.

## How to Find Your Invoices

1. Sign in to AcmeFlow Workspace.
2. Open **Workspace Settings → Billing**.
3. Select **Invoices** to view the list of generated invoices.
4. Open any invoice to view its details and download a copy.

Invoices remain available for the subscription lifetime, so you can return to them at any time.

## Receipts vs Invoices

- A **receipt** is available for each payment and confirms that a payment was received.
- An **invoice** is the billing statement for the cycle and includes the line-item breakdown.
- Both are available in the Workspace Billing section.

## Example: Reading an Invoice

**Example:** The invoice AF-2026-004821 for a Pro workspace covers a full monthly cycle for 8 seats at $24.00 per seat, for a base charge of $192.00, plus applicable tax. The invoice shows the period covered, the payment method charged, and the total due. The line items confirm the seat count and base charge match the subscription.

## Invoice Delivery Timing and the Billing Contact

The notification is sent to the billing contact on file. If you need a different person to receive invoice notifications, the billing contact can be updated in the Workspace.
