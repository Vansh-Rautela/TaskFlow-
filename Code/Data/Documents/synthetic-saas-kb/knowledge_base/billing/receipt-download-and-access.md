# Receipt Download & Access

This article explains how to access and download receipts for AcmeFlow Workspace payments. A receipt confirms that a payment was received and is available for each payment your workspace makes.

## What a Receipt Shows

A receipt is issued for each payment and typically shows:

- The receipt reference number (for example, `AF-RCPT-XXXXXX`)
- The payment date
- The amount charged
- The currency
- The payment method used
- The invoice it relates to

Receipts are distinct from invoices: the invoice is the billing statement for the cycle, while the receipt confirms the payment itself.

## How to Download a Receipt

1. Sign in to AcmeFlow Workspace.
2. Open **Workspace Settings → Billing**.
3. Select **Invoices** (receipts are linked to the payments on your invoices).
4. Open the relevant invoice and select **Receipt** to view the payment receipt.
5. Select **Download** to save a copy.

If you are looking for a specific payment, receipts can be located from the invoice of the cycle in which the payment posted.

## Receipt Availability

- A receipt is available for each payment as soon as the charge posts.
- Receipts remain available for the subscription lifetime, like invoices.
- If you need a receipt for a refund, it is listed as a separate receipt on the refund transaction.

**Example:** A Pro workspace paid $192.00 on its monthly cycle close. The receipt AF-RCPT-103482 confirms the payment of $192.00 in USD on that date, charged to the card ending in 4821, and is linked to the invoice for that cycle.

## Multiple Payments in a Cycle

A single cycle may include more than one payment, for example:

- The base subscription charge
- A prorated charge for added seats or an upgrade
- A partial payment after a dunning recovery

Each payment generates its own receipt, all linked to the same cycle's invoice.

## Payment Method References on Receipts

Receipts reference the payment method used, such as the last four digits of a card. This helps you match a receipt to a bank statement line.

**Example:** The bank statement shows a charge from `ACMEFLOW`. A receipt with the matching date and amount, referencing the card ending in 4821, confirms which workspace payment it relates to.
