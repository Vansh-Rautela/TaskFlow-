# Billing Address Changes

This article explains how to update the billing address on your AcmeFlow Workspace and how a change affects your invoices, tax treatment, and future charges.

## Why the Billing Address Matters

The billing address on file is used for:

- Displaying the address on your invoices and billing statements
- Tax calculation for the applicable jurisdiction
- Matching against the payment method's registered address
- Compliance and record keeping

An accurate billing address keeps your invoices correct and helps prevent declined payments.

## How to Update the Billing Address

1. Sign in to AcmeFlow Workspace.
2. Open **Workspace Settings → Billing**.
3. Select **Billing Address** (sometimes shown as **Company Details** or **Address**).
4. Enter the updated address and confirm.

The new address applies to invoices generated after the change. Invoices already generated are not re-issued to reflect the new address.

## What Changes with a New Billing Address

- **Invoices** — New invoices show the updated address.
- **Tax treatment** — Tax is calculated for the billing country, billing address, and applicable jurisdiction. Changing your address may change tax on future invoices.
- **Payments** — Ensure the address matches the payment method's registered address to avoid card verification issues.

**Example:** A Pro workspace in the US updates its billing address from New York to Texas. Future invoices show the Texas address and, where applicable, reflect the sales tax treatment for the new state. Existing invoices keep their original details.

## Changing Billing Country

Changing the billing country can affect:

- The currency and available payment methods for the workspace
- Tax treatment under the new jurisdiction
- Whether certain payment methods remain available

Not every currency or payment method is available in every region. If a change is needed, confirm the workspace settings after the update.

**Example:** A workspace moves from the United States to India and updates its billing country. Its invoices are now issued with the India billing address and, where applicable, use GST terminology for tax line items. The tax shown reflects the applicable jurisdiction's rules and is not a published AcmeFlow rate.

## Billing Address vs Billing Contact

- The **billing address** is the address on your invoices and used for tax.
- The **billing contact** is the person who receives invoice notifications by email.
- The two can be different and are updated separately in the Workspace.

## What Support Should Verify

When a customer reports incorrect address details on an invoice, the agent should verify the workspace, the invoice, and the billing address on file at the time the invoice was generated, rather than assuming the address was updated.
