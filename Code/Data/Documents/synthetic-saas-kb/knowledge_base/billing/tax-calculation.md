# Tax Calculation

This policy describes how taxes are calculated on AcmeFlow Workspace charges. Tax treatment varies by customer, location, and jurisdiction, and there is no single universal tax rate.

## Types of Tax Supported

AcmeFlow Workspace supports the following types of indirect tax where they apply:

- **US sales tax** — Applies to eligible US customers based on state and local requirements.
- **VAT (Value Added Tax)** — Applies in jurisdictions that use VAT, including many European countries.
- **GST (Goods and Services Tax)** — Applies in jurisdictions that use GST, including India.
- **Regional indirect taxes** — Other regional indirect taxes where applicable.

## What Determines Whether Tax Applies

Whether tax is charged, and at what rate, depends on:

- The workspace's **billing country**
- The **billing address** on file
- Whether the customer has provided a valid **tax registration** (for example, a VAT or GST registration number)
- The customer's **tax status** (for example, whether they are tax-exempt)
- The applicable **jurisdiction**

There is no single universal rate. Different customers in different locations will see different tax treatment, and a customer may have no tax charged where the jurisdiction does not require it.

## Providing Your Tax Registration

Where a tax registration number is available, provide it so it can be considered in tax calculation:

1. Sign in to AcmeFlow Workspace.
2. Open **Workspace Settings → Billing**.
3. Select **Tax Details**.
4. Enter your tax registration number and confirm.

A valid registration may change how tax is applied in the applicable jurisdiction. Support agents should verify the registration document status when a customer disputes tax.

## Tax on Invoices

- Taxes appear as a line item on invoices where applicable.
- Invoice line items reflect the subscription base charge, prorations, seat changes, taxes, and credits where applicable.
- The tax shown is calculated for the billing address and jurisdiction on file at the time the invoice is generated.

## Example: GST Terminology for an India Example

In India, tax is referred to as GST. There is no single universal GST percentage; the applicable rate depends on the jurisdiction and the details on file.

**Example:** An AcmeFlow Pro workspace with a billing address in India is charged GST on its subscription where applicable under the applicable jurisdiction's rules. The invoice for the cycle lists the base charge, a GST line, and the total. The GST rate shown is specific to that jurisdiction and is not a published AcmeFlow rate.

## Example: US Sales Tax

**Example:** A Pro workspace with 8 monthly seats has a base charge of $192.00. A US sales tax amount, where applicable for the workspace's billing address and state, is added as a separate line on the invoice. The sales tax rate shown is determined by the applicable state and local jurisdiction; it is not a published AcmeFlow rate.

## Billing Address and Tax

Tax is calculated using the billing address on file. Keeping the billing address accurate is important, because changing it can change the tax treatment on future invoices.

## Policy Exceptions

- The customer's signed Enterprise agreement takes precedence over the standard self-service policy where the two differ.
- Enterprise agreements may define contract currency, tax handling, and invoicing terms.
