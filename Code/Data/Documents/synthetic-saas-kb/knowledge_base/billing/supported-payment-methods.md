# Supported Payment Methods

AcmeFlow Workspace accepts a range of payment methods for subscription charges. The methods available to you depend on your plan, your region, and your account eligibility. Not every method is available on every plan or in every country, so the options shown in your Workspace Billing section are the authoritative list for your account.

## Payment Methods We Support

The following payment methods may be supported:

- **Credit cards** — Visa, Mastercard, and American Express are widely accepted. Discover is accepted where supported.
- **Debit cards** — Debit cards are accepted where supported.
- **ACH (bank transfer)** — Available to eligible US customers for subscription payments.
- **PayPal** — Available where PayPal is enabled for your account and region.
- **Wire transfer** — Available to qualifying Enterprise customers, typically under a signed agreement.

Availability is determined at the account and workspace level. If you do not see a method listed, it is not available for your plan or region.

## Where to See Your Available Methods

1. Sign in to AcmeFlow Workspace.
2. Open **Workspace Settings → Billing**.
3. Under **Payment Method**, review the options shown.
4. To switch methods, select the option you want to use and follow the prompts.

The methods listed here reflect what your plan, region, and eligibility allow. For example, an Enterprise workspace with a signed agreement may be eligible for wire transfer while a Pro workspace may not.

## Card Payments (Credit and Debit)

Card payments are the most common way to pay for AcmeFlow Workspace. When you add a card, we store only the card details needed to process and bill securely.

Things to know about card payments:

- Cards are charged at each billing cycle close for the amount shown on your invoice.
- The bank statement descriptor is `ACMEFLOW`, so the charge is recognizable on your statement.
- Card networks and payment processors apply market exchange rates when a charge is made in a currency different from the card's currency. There is no fixed conversion rate.
- Keep the card's billing address current so charges do not fail.

**Example:** A Pro workspace pays monthly with a Visa card ending in 4821. Each monthly cycle closes with a charge for the workspace's subscription total, and the payment appears on the card statement as `ACMEFLOW`.

## ACH (US Customers)

Eligible US customers can pay by ACH bank transfer. ACH is set up once, and then subscription charges are initiated automatically each cycle.

Things to know about ACH:

- Available only to eligible US customers whose account is set up for ACH.
- ACH refunds, when applicable, typically take 7–10 business days to appear once processed.
- Keep your bank account information current to avoid missed payments.

**Example:** A US-based Pro workspace on an annual plan pays by ACH. When the annual renewal closes, the charge is initiated through ACH and the workspace receives its invoice notification as usual.

## PayPal

PayPal is available where it is enabled for your account and region.

Things to know about PayPal:

- When paying by PayPal, you may be asked to authorize the payment through your PayPal account.
- PayPal refunds, when applicable, typically take 5–7 business days to appear.
- If the PayPal authorization fails or the balance is insufficient, the payment may fail and the standard dunning process begins.

## Wire Transfer (Enterprise)

Wire transfer is available to qualifying Enterprise customers, typically as part of a signed Enterprise agreement.

Things to know about wire transfers:

- Eligibility and instructions are defined per agreement.
- Wire payments may be on custom terms, including purchase orders or custom invoices.
- The customer's signed Enterprise agreement takes precedence over the standard self-service policy where the two differ.

## Payment Method Not Showing Up

If a payment method you expect is not listed, the most common reasons are:

- The method is not supported in your region.
- The method is not available on your plan.
- Your account does not meet the eligibility requirements (for example, ACH eligibility for US customers).
- For Enterprise terms, the method is defined by the signed agreement.

Support agents should confirm which plan the workspace is on and check the workspace region before troubleshooting.
