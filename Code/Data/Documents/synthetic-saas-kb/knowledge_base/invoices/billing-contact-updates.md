# Billing Contact Updates

This article explains what a billing contact is, how to change the billing contact for your AcmeFlow Workspace, and what to expect after you make the change.

## What Is a Billing Contact?

The billing contact is the person on your workspace who receives invoice notifications by email. When an invoice is generated at the close of a billing cycle, a notification is emailed to the workspace billing contact. The billing contact does not have to be an admin on the workspace, but the person must be able to act on billing matters, such as reviewing invoices or arranging payment.

The billing contact is separate from the people who manage day-to-day workflows in the workspace. It is specifically the address AcmeFlow uses for billing-related email.

## Before You Begin

- The workspace billing contact is managed in the Workspace Billing section.
- You need permission to change billing settings for the workspace. If you cannot access the Billing section, contact a workspace owner or admin.
- Have the new email address ready and confirm it is correct before saving.

## How to Update Your Billing Contact

1. Sign in to AcmeFlow Workspace.
2. Open **Workspace Settings → Billing**.
3. Find the **Billing Contact** field.
4. Enter the new email address for the billing contact.
5. Review the other billing details on the page (payment method, billing address, and invoice references) while you are there.
6. Save the change.

The updated address is used for billing notifications going forward. You should also make sure the new billing contact has access to the payment method if any payment action is needed.

## What Happens After You Update

- Invoice notifications are emailed to the new billing contact starting with the next notification sent.
- Invoices remain available in the Workspace Billing section for the subscription lifetime, so the new contact can view past invoices there even if earlier notifications went to the old address.
- Receipts are available in the Billing section for each payment.
- Past invoices are not re-sent to the new address automatically; use the Billing section to view or download them.

If you change the billing contact close to a renewal date, the notification for that cycle may still go to the previous address depending on when the invoice was generated. Check the Billing section if you do not receive the notification at the expected time.

## Example: Moving Invoices to a New Contact

Brightpath Logistics changes its billing contact from finance@brightpath.example to billing@brightpath.example on the 15th of the month. The next invoice notification, generated at the following cycle close, is emailed to billing@brightpath.example. The team views the current invoice in the Billing section in the meantime.

## Example: Updating the Contact Before an Annual Renewal

Northwind Studios is on an annual plan renewing on June 1. The billing team updates the billing contact in May so the annual renewal notification goes to the new address. Because the change is saved before the renewal invoice is generated, the June notification is sent to the new billing contact.

## Billing Contact Notifications

Invoices are generated at each billing cycle close and are available in the Workspace Billing section. The invoice notification is emailed to the workspace billing contact. Monthly invoices are generally available the day the charge posts; the exact posting time varies. If the billing contact has not received a notification, first confirm the email address is current in the Billing section and check spam or filtering rules.
