# Trial Period Terms

New AcmeFlow Workspace customers can evaluate the Pro plan with a **14-day trial**. This article explains how the trial works, what happens when it ends, and how to cancel before being charged.

## Standard Pro Trial

The standard trial gives access to **Pro plan features for 14 days**:

- No charge during the trial period.
- The trial applies to Pro features such as unlimited workflows, advanced permissions, audit logs, and standard analytics.
- Trial duration is 14 days for the standard self-service signup.

## Conversion at the End of the Trial

At the end of the 14-day trial, the workspace **converts to the selected paid subscription** per the signup terms:

- The customer selects a paid plan (for example, monthly or annual Pro) during signup or during the trial.
- The selected plan becomes active automatically when the trial ends.
- The first charge is the plan price for the selected billing frequency: **$24 per user per month** for monthly Pro, or **$20 per user per month** effective rate billed annually in advance.

The workspace's seat count at conversion determines the first paid invoice.

## Cancelling Before Conversion

Customers may cancel before the trial converts, and **no charge is applied**:

- Self-serve cancellation is available in Workspace Settings → Billing → Manage Subscription → Cancel Subscription.
- Cancelling during the trial prevents conversion and any charge.
- Trial access continues until the cancellation is processed; after cancelling, the workspace reverts to the Free plan limits.
- No invoice is generated for the trial period because there is no billable amount.

## Payment Method Capture

- Depending on the signup flow, AcmeFlow may capture a payment method at signup so conversion can proceed without interruption.
- Capturing a payment method does **not** create a charge during the trial.
- No charge is made until conversion.
- If no valid payment method is on file at the end of the trial, conversion may fail and the workspace may be restricted per the standard failed-payment process. See "Failed Payment & Dunning Process".

## Expiration and What Happens If the Trial Ends

- The trial ends at the conclusion of the 14-day period.
- If the workspace converts, the paid plan starts immediately and the first invoice is generated at the next billing cycle close.
- If a customer wants to keep using AcmeFlow but does not want to convert, they should cancel before the trial ends. Otherwise, they can downgrade to Free, which takes effect at the start of the next billing cycle per "Plan Upgrade & Downgrade Rules".

## Promotional and Enterprise Exceptions

- Trial durations other than 14 days are only available as a **documented promotional or Enterprise exception**; they are never standard.
- If a customer claims a longer trial, the agent must verify the promotion or the Enterprise agreement before confirming the extended date.
- The customer's signed Enterprise agreement takes precedence over the standard self-service policy where the two differ.

## Examples

**Example — conversion.** Kettlehouse & Co. signs up for a Pro trial on February 3. The trial ends February 17. At signup they selected monthly Pro. On February 17 the workspace converts to monthly Pro, and with 6 seats the first charge is 6 × $24 = **$144.00**, invoiced at the next billing cycle close (invoice `AF-2026-004821`).

**Example — cancel before conversion.** Northwind Labs starts a Pro trial on March 10 and decides AcmeFlow is not the right fit. They cancel in Workspace Settings on March 14. No charge is applied, no invoice is generated, and the workspace reverts to Free plan limits.

**Example — promotional exception.** AcmeFlow runs a documented 30-day promotional trial during a specific campaign. A customer who signed up during that promotion receives the full 30 days. The extended duration is tied to the promotion; the standard 14-day trial still applies to all other signups.
