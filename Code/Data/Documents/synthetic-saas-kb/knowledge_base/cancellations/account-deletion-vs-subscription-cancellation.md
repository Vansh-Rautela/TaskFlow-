# Account Deletion vs Subscription Cancellation

Cancelling your subscription and deleting your account are two different actions in AcmeFlow Workspace. This article explains the difference and how to handle each one correctly.

## Cancelling Stops Future Renewal

Cancelling your subscription stops future renewal. Your paid access continues until the end of the current billing period, and your workspace and data remain in place. Cancelling does not delete your data.

If your goal is simply to stop being charged, cancellation is the correct action. See Self-Serve Cancellation.

## Deleting Closes the Account

Deleting (or closing) your account removes the account itself and its data. Deletion is a separate action from cancellation and is more permanent. Once your data is deleted, it may not be possible to restore it.

Because deletion is irreversible, plan carefully before you close an account.

## Your Data Is Retained for 60 Days

Workspace data is retained for 60 days after your subscription terminates. This retention window gives you time to export the data you need or to change your mind and reactivate.

Important points about the window:

- The 60 days are counted from the date the subscription terminates.
- During the window, the workspace data is not immediately removed.
- After the retention period, deletion may be irreversible.
- If you plan to close the account, export your data before you delete it. See Data Export Before Cancellation.

## Recommended Order of Actions

If you want to close your account permanently:

1. Cancel your subscription. See Self-Serve Cancellation.
2. Export the data you need. See Data Export Before Cancellation.
3. Delete the account once the export is complete.

Cancelling first stops new charges; exporting first protects your data; deleting last closes the account.

## Examples

**Example 1 — Cancel only:** The Bluepeak Design workspace wants to stop paying but keep the ability to come back. The admin cancels the subscription. Access continues to the end of the billing period, the data is retained for 60 days, and the workspace is not deleted.

**Example 2 — Cancel and delete:** The Harbor & Co. workspace is closing permanently. The admin cancels the subscription, exports workflows and reports, and then deletes the account. Once deleted, the data cannot be restored.

## What If I Deleted by Accident?

If you believe an account was deleted or data is missing, do not assume data can be recovered. Support agents should retrieve the workspace's actual termination and deletion status from the account system rather than guessing. Data deleted after the retention period may be irreversible.
