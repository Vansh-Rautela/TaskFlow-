# Cancellation Effective Date

This policy defines when a cancellation takes effect, how access and billing behave during the current billing period, and how cancellation interacts with downgrades and refunds.

## When Cancellation Takes Effect

Cancelling a subscription prevents future renewal. The cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing period, not on the date the cancellation is confirmed.

The current billing period is the period already paid for and in progress at the time of cancellation. For a monthly subscription, this is the current month of service. For an annual subscription, this is the current year of service.

## Paid Access Continues Until the End of the Billing Period

Paid access continues until the end of the current billing period. Customers keep full access to the features of their plan for the remainder of the period they have paid for. Access stops when the subscription terminates at the end of that period.

**Example:** A Pro workspace on monthly billing has a billing period that runs from June 1 through June 30. The admin cancels on June 18. Access continues through June 30, the subscription does not renew on July 1, and access stops on July 1.

## Monthly Renewal Dates

Renewal occurs on the same calendar day as the original subscription date. If that day does not exist in a month, the renewal is billed on the final day of that month. The cancellation effective date is aligned to this renewal schedule.

## Downgrades and Effective Dates

A plan downgrade takes effect at the start of the next billing cycle. Unused time from the current cycle is not automatically refunded unless a policy explicitly allows it. A downgrade is therefore applied from the next cycle, and the customer continues on the higher plan until then.

Cancellation is treated the same way: it takes effect at the end of the current billing period. There is no prorated credit for the period between cancellation confirmation and the period end under the standard policy.

## Refunds and Unused Time

Cancellation does not automatically create a refund. Unused time in the current billing period is not automatically refunded. Refunds are governed by the Standard 30-Day Refund Policy and are only granted where the applicable policy explicitly permits them. Prorated refunds require explicit policy permission. See Prorated Refund Calculation and Refund Exception Approval.

## Annual Subscriptions

For annual subscriptions, cancellation takes effect at the end of the annual period. The customer keeps access for the remainder of the paid year and is not charged at the next anniversary.

**Example:** A workspace on the Pro plan billed annually (effective rate $20 per user per month, billed annually in advance) with 10 Pro seats is charged $2,400 on the January 15 anniversary (10 users × $20 × 12 months). The admin cancels on September 3. Access continues through January 14 of the following year, and no renewal charge is raised at the next anniversary.

## Enterprise Overrides

Enterprise contracts may define different cancellation effective dates, refund terms, or billing arrangements. The customer's signed Enterprise agreement takes precedence over the standard self-service policy where the two differ. Support agents should refer to the signed agreement for Enterprise accounts and must not apply the standard policy without checking.
