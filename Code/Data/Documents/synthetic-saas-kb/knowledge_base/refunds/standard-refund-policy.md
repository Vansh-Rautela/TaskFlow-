# Standard 30-Day Refund Policy

## Purpose

This policy defines when an AcmeFlow Workspace charge is eligible for a refund and how the 30-day refund window works. It applies to self-service plans (Free and Pro) unless a signed agreement states otherwise.

## Refund Window

- A charge is eligible for a standard refund for **30 days from the original charge date**.
- The window is measured from the date the charge posted, not from the date the invoice was opened, the subscription started, or the payment cleared in the customer's bank.
- Refund requests received after the window closes are not covered by this policy. They may only proceed through the Refund Exception Approval policy.

## Cancellation Does Not Create a Refund

- Cancelling a subscription does **not** automatically create a refund.
- Unused time remaining in a paid month is **not automatically refundable**.
- Paid access continues until the end of the current billing period, so cancelling mid-cycle does not release a partial refund by itself.
- A separate, refundable charge (for example, a charge posted within the last 30 days) is still subject to the 30-day window in this policy even if the subscription is later cancelled.

## What Is Eligible

Eligible under the standard policy:

- Pro seat charges posted within the last 30 days.
- Prorated charges generated by mid-cycle seat additions, when posted within the last 30 days.

Not eligible under the standard policy:

- Charges older than 30 days (exceptions only).
- Amounts that have already been refunded or reversed.
- Tax treatment, which is determined per jurisdiction and covered in the Tax Calculation policy.
- Charges covered by different refund terms in a signed Enterprise agreement.

## How Refunds Are Issued

- An eligible refund is returned to the original payment method where AcmeFlow can do so.
- When a refund is issued as credit, or when the original payment method is no longer available, the amount is held as a workspace credit balance. Credit balances are governed by the Credit Balance Application policy.
- Processing time varies by payment method. Timeframes are estimates, not guarantees; see the Refund Processing Timeframes policy.

## Enterprise Contracts

- Enterprise contracts may contain different refund terms.
- The customer's signed Enterprise agreement takes precedence over the standard self-service policy where the two differ.

## Worked Examples

### Example 1 — Request within the 30-day window

Northwind Consulting operates an 8-seat Pro workspace billed monthly at $24 per user. Each monthly charge is $192.00 (8 × $24). The charge for invoice AF-2026-004821 posted on Feb 3, 2026. The customer contacted support on Feb 20, 2026 — 17 days after the charge posted. The request falls within the 30-day window, and the charge is eligible for a standard refund.

### Example 2 — Request outside the 30-day window

Long Tail Labs paid $192.00 on invoice AF-2026-003960, with the charge posting on Jan 15, 2026. A refund request arrived on Feb 25, 2026 — 41 days after the charge posted. The request is outside the standard 30-day window and is not eligible under this policy. It may only proceed as an exception under the Refund Exception Approval policy.

## Information Required to Process a Refund

To assess a refund request, an agent must retrieve from the customer's billing record, or ask the customer to confirm:

- Invoice ID (for example, AF-2026-004821)
- The original charge date and charge amount
- The plan and number of seats on the charge
- The billing cycle (monthly or annual)
- The payment method used

These account-specific details are verified against the billing record; they are never assumed.
