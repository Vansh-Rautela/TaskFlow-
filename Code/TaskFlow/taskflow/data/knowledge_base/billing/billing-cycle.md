# Billing Cycle Explanation

This policy describes how billing cycles work for AcmeFlow Workspace subscriptions, including renewal dates, cycle timing, and how changes to your plan or seat count affect when and how much you are billed.

## What a Billing Cycle Is

A billing cycle is the recurring period covered by a subscription payment. At the close of each cycle, AcmeFlow Workspace generates an invoice for the period and charges the workspace's payment method.

Each invoice covers one full cycle unless a change in the middle of the cycle (an upgrade, a proration, or a seat change) requires additional line items.

## Monthly Billing Cycle

Monthly subscriptions renew on the same calendar day as the original subscription date.

- If the renewal day does not exist in a month (for example, the 31st of a month with only 30 days), the workspace is billed on the final day of that month.
- Monthly invoices are generally available the day the charge posts; exact posting time varies.

**Example:** A Pro workspace is subscribed on January 31. The February renewal is billed on February 28 (the final day of February), and the March renewal returns to March 31.

## Annual Billing Cycle

Annual subscriptions renew on the anniversary date of the original subscription.

- Annual pricing is billed annually in advance at the effective annual rate.
- The anniversary renewal uses the same calendar date as the original annual start date.

**Example:** A Pro workspace starts an annual plan on March 15, 2026. Its next annual renewal is March 15, 2027.

## Plan Changes and Their Effect on the Cycle

- **Plan upgrade** — Takes effect immediately. Additional charges may be prorated for the remainder of the current cycle.
- **Plan downgrade** — Takes effect at the start of the next billing cycle. Unused time on the higher plan is not automatically refunded unless a policy explicitly allows it.
- **Adding seats** — Adding seats during an active cycle may generate a prorated charge for the rest of the cycle.
- **Removing seats** — Removing seats normally affects future billing only; the current cycle is not credited automatically unless a policy explicitly permits it.

The billing cycle dates themselves do not reset when a plan change occurs; the change is layered onto the existing cycle.

**Example:** A Pro workspace on monthly billing adds 2 seats mid-cycle. A prorated charge for the added seats is added to the next invoice, and the full new seat count is billed starting with the next cycle.

## Seat Change Calculations

- Additional seats added mid-cycle are charged on a prorated basis from the effective date through the end of the cycle.
- Removed seats reduce the charge from the next cycle forward.
- Invoice line items reflect subscription base charges, prorations, seat changes, taxes, and credits where applicable.

## Example Plan Math

Monthly Pro pricing is $24 per user per month; annual Pro pricing is $20 per user per month (effective rate, billed annually in advance).

**Example:** A Pro workspace with 8 seats on monthly billing is charged $192.00 (8 × $24.00) at each monthly cycle close.

## Billing Contact Notifications

- An invoice notification is emailed to the workspace billing contact at each cycle close.
- Invoices are also available in the Workspace Billing section.
- Receipts are available for payment; invoices remain available for the subscription lifetime.

## Policy Exceptions

- The customer's signed Enterprise agreement takes precedence over the standard self-service policy where the two differ.
- Enterprise agreements may define custom billing periods, including quarterly billing, contract-specific seat commitments, and contract currency.
