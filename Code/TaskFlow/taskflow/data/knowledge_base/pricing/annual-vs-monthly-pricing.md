# Annual vs Monthly Pricing

Pro subscriptions on AcmeFlow Workspace may be billed **monthly** or **annually**. The choice affects the per-user price, the amount and timing of each charge, and how changes like upgrades or downgrades behave. This document explains both options and how to switch between them.

## Prices and Effective Rate

- **Monthly billing**: **$24 per user per month**, charged each billing cycle.
- **Annual billing**: billed **annually in advance** at an effective rate of **$20 per user per month**, meaning the full year's amount is charged up front.

The annual option is cheaper on a per-user basis. The effective rate is not a separate monthly price; the annual amount for each seat is $20 × 12 = **$240 per user per year**, charged once per year.

## Billing Timing

- **Monthly renewal**: occurs on the same calendar day as the original subscription date; if that day does not exist in a month, the charge posts on the final day of that month.
- **Annual renewal**: occurs on the anniversary date of the original subscription.

Invoices are generated at each billing cycle close and are available in the Workspace Billing section. See "Billing Cycle Explanation" and "Invoice Generation & Delivery" for details.

## Comparing Total Cost

| Seats | Monthly (per month) | Annual (billed once per year) |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | $24.00 | $240.00 |
| 5 | $120.00 | $1,200.00 |
| 10 | $240.00 | $2,400.00 |
| 25 | $600.00 | $6,000.00 |

The monthly column shows the per-cycle charge; the annual column shows the single up-front charge for the year.

## Upgrades and Downgrades

- **Upgrading a plan** (for example, moving a workspace to a higher tier) takes effect immediately, and additional charges may be **prorated**.
- **Downgrading a plan** takes effect at the start of the next billing cycle, and unused time on the higher tier is not automatically refunded unless the applicable policy explicitly permits it.
- Seat changes follow the same rules: adding seats mid-cycle may generate a prorated charge, and removing seats affects future billing only.

These rules are the same for monthly and annual subscriptions. For annual plans, "next billing cycle" means the next annual anniversary date.

## Switching Billing Frequency

**Monthly to annual** is treated as an upgrade:

- Takes effect immediately.
- The annual amount for the new billing frequency may be prorated relative to the current cycle, and the remainder of the month's monthly charge is reconciled accordingly.
- The workspace is then billed annually in advance at $20 per user per month effective.

**Annual to monthly** is treated as a downgrade:

- Takes effect at the start of the next billing cycle (the next annual anniversary date).
- The current annual term continues unchanged until then.
- Unused time on the annual term is not automatically refunded unless the applicable policy explicitly permits it.

## Enterprise Billing Frequency

Enterprise billing frequency is defined by the signed agreement and is commonly annual or quarterly. The customer's signed Enterprise agreement takes precedence over the standard self-service policy where the two differ. Enterprise prices are custom and never quoted as standard.

## Examples

**Example — annual up-front charge.** Brightpath converts 10 seats to annual Pro. The one-time annual charge is 10 × $240 = **$2,400.00**, billed in advance and invoiced as `AF-2026-004821`. Renewal occurs on the anniversary date each year.

**Example — switching monthly to annual.** Meridian Health Group is on monthly Pro with 10 seats at $24 per seat. They switch to annual mid-cycle. The switch takes effect immediately, the annual amount is reconciled against the current cycle, and the workspace begins paying 10 × $240 = **$2,400.00** per year.

**Example — switching annual to monthly.** Greenfield Media is on annual Pro and requests a switch to monthly on March 5. Because the change is a downgrade, the switch takes effect at the start of the next billing cycle on the anniversary date. Until then, their annual term continues unchanged, and no refund is issued for the unused portion of the annual term.
