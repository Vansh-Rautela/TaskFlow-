# Feature Matrix by Plan

This document compares the features and limits of the three AcmeFlow Workspace plans: **Free**, **Pro**, and **Enterprise**. Use it to answer "what does each plan include?" questions and to set expectations when a customer is deciding between tiers.

Enterprise cells are intentionally general because Enterprise terms are set per signed agreement. Never quote an Enterprise feature or limit as universal; the customer's signed Enterprise agreement takes precedence over the standard self-service policy where the two differ.

## Comparison Table

| Feature | Free | Pro | Enterprise |
|---|---|---|---|
| Price | $0 per month (never charged) | $24/user/month monthly, or $20/user/month effective rate billed annually in advance | Custom, per signed agreement |
| Users (seats) | Up to 3 | Per user, no self-service cap | Contract-specific seat commitments |
| Active workflows | 2 | Unlimited | Unlimited (per agreement) |
| Workflow executions | 500 per month | Up to 10,000 per month per workspace | Per agreement |
| Permissions | Basic roles | Advanced permissions | Advanced permissions with contract-specific controls |
| Audit logs | Not included | Included | Included with advanced audit controls |
| Analytics | Not included | Standard analytics | Per agreement |
| Integrations | Standard integrations | Standard integrations | Standard integrations plus contract-specific options |
| SSO / SAML | Not included | Not included | Included |
| SCIM | Not included | Not included | Included |
| Support | Community support | Email support | Dedicated account manager (per agreement) |
| SLA | None | None | Custom SLA; standard target 99.9% monthly uptime |
| Custom invoices | Not available | Not available | Available (including purchase orders) |
| Billing frequency | Never billed | Monthly or annual | Annual, quarterly, or per agreement |

## Pricing Notes

- Pro monthly is **$24 per user per month**.
- Pro annual is billed annually in advance at an effective rate of **$20 per user per month**.
- Enterprise pricing is custom and negotiated. Never present an Enterprise price as standard; any Enterprise figure in an example must be labeled as an example.

See "Annual vs Monthly Pricing" for the difference between the two Pro billing frequencies, and "Plan Tiers Overview" for the intended customer for each tier.

## Workflow and Execution Limits

- **Free**: 2 active workflows and 500 workflow executions per month. There are no add-ons on the Free plan; exceeding these limits requires an upgrade.
- **Pro**: Unlimited workflows and up to 10,000 workflow executions per month per workspace.
- **Enterprise**: Limits are set per agreement. If an Enterprise agreement differs from the Pro limits, the agreement governs.

Workflow executions are counted per workspace per calendar month. See "Why Was I Charged?" if a customer asks how executions are counted on an invoice.

## Users, Seats, and Permissions

- **Free**: up to 3 users with basic roles.
- **Pro**: per-user pricing with advanced permissions and audit logs.
- **Enterprise**: contract-specific seat commitments with SSO/SAML, SCIM, and advanced audit controls.

For seat math, proration, and add-on rules, see "Seat Limits & Add-Ons".

## Analytics and Reporting

- **Free**: no analytics feature.
- **Pro**: standard analytics included.
- **Enterprise**: analytics scope is defined per agreement.

## Integrations

Standard integrations are included on all plans. Enterprise agreements may add contract-specific integrations. There is no paid "integrations add-on" in self-service plans.

## Support and SLA

- **Free**: community support, no SLA.
- **Pro**: email support, no SLA.
- **Enterprise**: dedicated account manager and custom SLA per agreement. The standard Enterprise SLA target is 99.9% monthly uptime for AcmeFlow Workspace, with service credits for missed uptime defined per agreement.

## Billing Features

- Custom invoices, purchase orders, and contract-specific payment terms are Enterprise-only.
- Free and Pro workspaces receive standard invoices generated at each billing cycle close and available in the Workspace Billing section.

See "Invoice Generation & Delivery" and "Enterprise Custom Invoice Requirements" for details.

## Examples

**Example — Free vs Pro.** Cedar Peak Consulting has 4 users and needs 3 active workflows. The Free plan caps at 3 users and 2 active workflows, so the workspace upgrades to Pro. With 4 users billed monthly, the charge is 4 × $24 = **$96.00** per month.

**Example — Pro analytics and audit logs.** Meridian Health Group needs audit logs for internal compliance. Audit logs are included with Pro, so no separate add-on is required. They run 10 users on annual billing: 10 × $240 = **$2,400.00** billed annually in advance (invoice `AF-2026-004821`).

**Example — Enterprise capabilities (labeled example only).** Ardent & Sons signs an Enterprise agreement that includes SSO/SAML, SCIM, and a 99.9% SLA. These capabilities are part of the negotiated agreement and are not available as self-service add-ons.
