# Plan Tiers Overview

AcmeFlow Workspace is available in three plans: **Free**, **Pro**, and **Enterprise**. Each tier is designed for a different customer profile, from small teams getting started to large organizations with contract-specific requirements.

This document is the entry point for understanding what each plan includes, how it is priced, how often it is billed, and what level of support and features come with it. For a side-by-side comparison of individual features, see "Feature Matrix by Plan".

## The Three Plans at a Glance

| Plan | Intended customer | Pricing model | Billing frequency |
|---|---|---|---|
| Free | Startups and small teams evaluating AcmeFlow | $0 per month, never charged | Not applicable |
| Pro | Growing teams and small businesses | Per user per month | Monthly ($24/user) or annual ($20/user effective rate, billed annually in advance) |
| Enterprise | Mid-market and enterprise organizations | Custom, per signed agreement | Contract-specific (often annual or quarterly) |

## Free Plan

The Free plan is designed for evaluation and very small teams. It is **$0 per month** and is never charged.

Key limits and inclusions:

- Up to **3 users**.
- **2 active workflows**.
- **500 workflow executions per month**.
- **Community support** only.
- **Standard integrations**.
- **No SLA**.
- **No SSO/SAML**.
- **No custom invoices**.

If a workspace needs more users, workflows, or executions, it must move to a paid plan. Free plan capacity is shared per workspace; exceeding the limits requires an upgrade rather than an add-on.

## Pro Plan

The Pro plan is the standard paid self-service tier for growing teams.

Pricing and inclusions:

- **$24 per user per month** when billed monthly.
- **$20 per user per month** (effective rate) when billed annually, with the annual amount billed in advance.
- **Unlimited workflows**.
- **Advanced permissions**.
- **Audit logs**.
- **Standard integrations**.
- **Email support**.
- **Standard analytics**.
- Up to **10,000 workflow executions per month per workspace**.

Pro seats are priced per user, so the charge scales with the number of users in the workspace. Pro workspaces pay for every active user; there are no block minimums on the self-service tier.

## Enterprise Plan

The Enterprise plan is sold through a signed agreement rather than self-service checkout.

Enterprise features may include:

- **Volume discounts**.
- **Contract-specific seat commitments**.
- **Annual or quarterly billing**.
- **Purchase orders**.
- **Custom invoices**.
- **SSO/SAML** and **SCIM**.
- **Advanced audit controls**.
- **Dedicated account manager**.
- **Custom SLA**.
- **Security reviews**.
- **Contract-specific payment terms**.

Enterprise pricing is **custom** and negotiated per organization. AcmeFlow does not publish a universal Enterprise price. The customer's signed Enterprise agreement takes precedence over the standard self-service policy where the two differ.

## Billing Frequency

Billing frequency depends on the plan:

- **Free**: never billed.
- **Pro**: choose monthly or annual at signup or when upgrading.
- **Enterprise**: set by the signed agreement, commonly annual or quarterly.

Monthly Pro renews on the same calendar day as the original subscription date; if that day does not exist in a month, the renewal bills on the final day of that month. Annual Pro renews on its anniversary date. See "Billing Cycle Explanation" for details.

## Support Levels

- **Free**: community support. There is no SLA and no direct support mailbox.
- **Pro**: email support included with the plan. No SLA applies to self-service plans.
- **Enterprise**: a dedicated account manager and a custom SLA defined per agreement. The standard Enterprise SLA target is 99.9% monthly uptime for AcmeFlow Workspace.

## Major Feature Differences

- **Workflows**: Free allows 2 active workflows; Pro and Enterprise allow unlimited.
- **Users**: Free caps at 3 users; Pro bills per user with no cap; Enterprise follows the contract's seat commitment.
- **Executions**: Free includes 500 per month; Pro includes up to 10,000 per month per workspace; Enterprise is set per agreement.
- **Security**: SSO/SAML and SCIM are Enterprise-only. Audit logs are included on Pro and Enterprise.
- **Billing artifacts**: Custom invoices are Enterprise-only. Free and Pro receive standard invoices generated at each billing cycle close.
- **SLA**: Only Enterprise has an SLA; self-service plans (Free and Pro) have none.

## Examples

**Example — Free plan evaluation.** Bright Spark Studio signs up for the Free plan with 3 users. They never receive a charge, and no invoice is generated because there is no billable amount. They evaluate AcmeFlow for two weeks before upgrading to Pro.

**Example — Pro monthly billing.** Northwind Labs runs the Pro plan with 8 users billed monthly. The monthly charge is 8 × $24 = **$192.00**, invoiced each cycle with an invoice number such as `AF-2026-004821`.

**Example — Enterprise custom terms (labeled example only).** Blue Harbor Logistics signs an Enterprise agreement at an example rate of $18 per user per month under a two-year term with a 150-seat commitment. This is a negotiated example and is not a published Enterprise price. The signed agreement governs billing, seats, and SLA.
