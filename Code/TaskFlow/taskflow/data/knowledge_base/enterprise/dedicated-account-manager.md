# Dedicated Account Manager

A dedicated account manager (DAM) is an option for Enterprise customers and is included where the signed agreement provides for it. The DAM acts as a single point of contact between the customer and AcmeFlow for onboarding, adoption, and operational coordination. Whether a specific account has a DAM, and the scope of that role, is defined in the agreement.

This document describes what a dedicated account manager does and how support agents should route work related to these accounts.

## Role of the Dedicated Account Manager

Where included, the dedicated account manager supports the customer across several areas:

- Coordinating onboarding and workspace setup under the enterprise agreement.
- Helping with feature adoption and workflow best practices.
- Serving as the escalation point for operational or billing questions.
- Coordinating renewals, seat changes, and contract questions with the customer's team.
- Liaising with Billing Operations and Enterprise Support on the customer's behalf.

The DAM does not replace support channels. Technical incidents, billing disputes, and product issues are still handled through the normal support and billing processes, with the DAM helping to coordinate when needed.

## Scope Is Set by the Agreement

The exact responsibilities and service levels of the dedicated account manager are set in the signed agreement. Account management support is not a standardized package that applies to every Enterprise customer.

**Example (labeled):** Under MSAA-2026-0417, BlueSky Analytics Inc. has a dedicated account manager who leads quarterly business reviews and acts as the escalation contact for billing questions. The quarterly review cadence is a term of that contract.

**Example (labeled):** Meridian Health Partners' agreement provides a dedicated account manager for onboarding and renewal coordination only; technical issues are routed to Enterprise Support directly without DAM involvement.

## How Support Agents Should Work With These Accounts

When a support agent receives a request from an enterprise account:

- Check whether the account has a dedicated account manager listed on the agreement.
- Coordinate with the DAM before making commitments about billing changes, seat adjustments, or contract terms.
- For escalation or renewal questions, involve the assigned DAM rather than resolving through the standard self-service flow.
- Retrieve account-specific entitlements from the agreement rather than assuming them.

The customer's signed Enterprise agreement takes precedence over the standard self-service policy where the two differ, including for account management and escalation processes.
