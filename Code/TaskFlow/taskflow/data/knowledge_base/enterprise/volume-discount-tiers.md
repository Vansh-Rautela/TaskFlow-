# Volume Discount Tiers

Volume discounts are available to Enterprise customers and are negotiated as part of the signed agreement. Because Enterprise pricing is custom, there is no published, universal set of volume-discount tiers that applies to every customer. Discount percentages and the volume levels at which they apply are established at contract signing and recorded in the agreement.

This document explains the structure of volume discounts and how to handle questions about them without assuming a standard schedule.

## How Volume Discounts Are Structured

Volume discounts are generally applied against either the number of committed seats or the size of the annual commitment. The exact structure is defined in a volume-discount table within the signed agreement.

Typical structures include:

- Discounts that step up as committed seats pass negotiated thresholds.
- Discounts tied to a minimum annual commitment.
- Discounts that apply to the subscription base charge, seat add-ons, or both.
- One-time negotiated rates that do not change with volume.

Because thresholds and percentages are contract-specific, an agent should never quote a standard tier. The discount applicable to an account must be read from the account's signed agreement.

## No Universal Tier Thresholds

AcmeFlow does not maintain a fixed schedule of volume-discount thresholds. A percentage and its triggering volume are meaningful only in the context of a specific contract.

If a support agent or customer needs a number, it must come from the agreement. Any figure presented without a contract reference is an example only and must be labeled as such.

**Example (labeled):** The volume-discount table for Optivus Manufacturing (contract MSAA-2026-0551) applies a 5% discount on committed seats at 200 seats and a 10% discount at 500 seats. The 5% and 10% figures are negotiated for that contract only, and the thresholds are not a standard schedule.

## How Discounts Are Applied at Billing Time

When a discount is negotiated, it is typically reflected in the contract rate or applied as a line-item adjustment on invoices. The mechanics depend on the agreement:

- Discounted per-seat rates may be locked for the term of the agreement.
- Seat changes during an active cycle may be billed at the prorated discounted rate.
- The discount may be conditioned on maintaining a minimum committed seat count.

**Example (labeled):** Under its agreement, Optivus Manufacturing maintains 220 committed seats. Its negotiated 5% discount continues to apply, and the discounted rate appears on invoice AF-2026-006412. If the committed count drops below 200 seats, the agreement requires AcmeFlow to re-apply the standard contract rate for the remainder of the term.

## Questions About Discounts

For any question about a discount percentage, threshold, or how it appears on an invoice, the agent must check the volume-discount table in the signed agreement before responding. Account-specific discount figures are never fabricated from memory.

If the agreement does not contain a volume-discount provision, the account is billed at the contracted rate without a volume adjustment, and the standard billing rules for the plan apply.
