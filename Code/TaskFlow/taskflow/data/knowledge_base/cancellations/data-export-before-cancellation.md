# Data Export Before Cancellation

If you are planning to cancel your AcmeFlow Workspace subscription or close your account, export the data you need first. This article explains why exports matter and how to prepare for cancellation.

## Why Export Your Data

Your workspace data is retained for 60 days after your subscription terminates, but that does not mean it is available forever. After the retention period, the data is subject to deletion, and deletion may be irreversible.

Exporting the data you need before you cancel or delete your account is the only way to keep your own copy. Cancellation does not automatically produce an export, and closing an account removes it.

## What You Can Export

Your workspace contains workflows, configurations, reports, and other data your team created. Before you cancel, review what your team relies on and export what you need to keep.

The export options available depend on your plan. Confirm the details for your workspace rather than assuming every data type is available. If you are not sure what an export contains, check the workspace export screens before confirming.

## How to Export

1. Sign in to AcmeFlow Workspace.
2. Open the workspace settings for your account.
3. Find the export option for the data you want to keep, such as workflows or reports.
4. Run the export and save the resulting files to a location you control.
5. Verify the exported files open and contain the data you expect.

If a specific data type is not available for export, note that before you cancel, because you may not be able to retrieve it later.

## Export Before Deletion, Not After

Export first, then delete. If you delete the account before you export, the data is removed with the account and cannot be restored. The recommended order is:

1. Cancel the subscription.
2. Export the data you need.
3. Delete the account once the export is complete.

See Account Deletion vs Subscription Cancellation for how these actions differ.

## Examples

**Example 1:** The Ridgefield Accounting workspace is migrating off AcmeFlow. The admin exports the workflows and reports on June 25, then cancels the subscription. The data remains available for reference until the 60-day retention window ends.

**Example 2:** A support agent is asked whether the Cascade Goods workspace can still recover a workflow that was never exported. The workspace cancelled on March 10 and the retention window ended in May. The agent retrieves the account's actual termination and retention status from the account system instead of promising recovery, because data deleted after the retention period may be irreversible.

## Agent Guidance

When a customer asks what data can be recovered or exported for their specific account, do not fabricate account-specific details. Retrieve the account's plan, subscription status, and termination date from the account system, then confirm what export options are available for that workspace.
