# Reactivation & Price Locking

This policy defines how a customer can return to a paid plan after cancellation, when workspace data is preserved, and when the customer may keep the per-user price they previously paid.

## Reactivation Overview

A customer who cancels a Pro subscription can return to the Pro plan at any time. Reactivation restores paid access and, when it occurs within the data retention window, restores the workspace data that was retained after the subscription terminated.

Reactivation can be started through the workspace billing flow. If the workspace is past the point where the self-serve flow is available, the customer or an agent should follow the applicable account workflow.

## Workspace Data Is Preserved During the Window

Workspace data is retained for 60 days after the subscription terminates. If a customer reactivates within that window, the retained data is restored to the workspace. Reactivating within the window means the customer does not need to rebuild workflows or re-upload data.

## The Price Lock Window

A customer who cancels and returns to the Pro plan within 60 days of cancellation may retain the same per-user price they previously paid. This is referred to as a price lock.

Policy details:

- The price lock is honored if Pro is re-purchased within 60 days of the cancellation date.
- The price lock retains the same per-user price the customer paid before cancellation.
- The price lock applies to the Pro plan. It is not available when re-purchasing under other arrangements.
- If re-purchase happens more than 60 days after cancellation, the price lock does not apply and the standard Pro pricing in effect at that time applies instead.

The 60-day price lock window aligns with the standard 60-day data retention window so that a customer returning within the window gets both their data and their previous per-user price.

## How the Price Lock Works by Billing Frequency

- If the customer was on monthly Pro billing, the price lock holds the per-user monthly price they previously paid.
- If the customer was on annual Pro billing, the price lock holds the per-user effective annual rate they previously paid, billed annually in advance.

**Example 1 (monthly):** Northwind Logistics was on the Pro plan at $24 per user per month with 8 users. They cancelled in June. On July 20 (within 60 days), they re-purchase Pro with 8 users. Because the price lock applies, they are billed at the same $24 per user per month, not at a higher current price. Their retained workspace data is restored with the reactivation.

**Example 2 (annual):** The Meridian Studio workspace was on the Pro plan at the effective rate of $20 per user per month, billed annually in advance. They cancelled and returned within the 60-day window with the same number of seats. The annual per-user rate is locked at $20 per user per month for the new annual period, and the retained data is restored.

## Price Lock Does Not Create a Refund

A price lock governs the price charged on reactivation. It does not create a refund for the cancelled period and does not change the standard refund policy. See Standard 30-Day Refund Policy.

## Enterprise Accounts

The price lock and 60-day retention described here are the standard self-service policy. Enterprise accounts are governed by their signed Enterprise agreement, which may define different pricing, renewal, or reactivation terms. The customer's signed Enterprise agreement takes precedence over the standard self-service policy where the two differ. Do not state the 60-day price lock or retention window as a universal Enterprise term.

## Agent Guidance

When a customer asks whether their previous price or data is still available after cancellation, retrieve the account's actual cancellation date, plan, and billing frequency from the billing system before answering. Do not assume a price lock or data restoration applies based on the customer's description alone.
