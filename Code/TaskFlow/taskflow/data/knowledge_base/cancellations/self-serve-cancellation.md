# Self-Serve Cancellation

You can cancel your AcmeFlow Workspace paid subscription at any time from your Workspace Settings. Cancellation is self-serve, which means you do not need to contact support or send a request by email. Once you confirm, your subscription will not renew at the end of the current billing period.

This article walks through the cancellation steps and explains what to expect after you cancel.

## Before You Cancel

Before you cancel, consider whether your team still needs access to AcmeFlow Workspace data.

- If you plan to close your account permanently, export any data you need before you delete the account. See Data Export Before Cancellation.
- Cancellation is different from account deletion. Your workspace data is retained for 60 days after your subscription terminates, but deletion after that window may be irreversible. See Account Deletion vs Subscription Cancellation.

## How to Cancel Your Subscription

1. Sign in to AcmeFlow Workspace.
2. Open **Workspace Settings → Billing**.
3. In the **Manage Subscription** section, select **Cancel Subscription**.
4. Review the cancellation details shown on the confirmation screen, including when access ends and what happens to your workspace.
5. Confirm the cancellation.

After you confirm, you will see a confirmation in the Billing section. A cancellation confirmation is also sent to the workspace billing contact by email.

## What Happens After You Cancel

When you cancel, the following applies:

- Your subscription will not renew at the end of the current billing period.
- Paid access to the features included in your plan continues until the end of the current billing period. You can keep using the workspace normally during that time.
- When the current billing period ends, paid access stops and your subscription is terminated.

**Example:** The Nordwind Logistics workspace is on the Pro plan, billed monthly from the 15th of each month. The billing admin cancels the subscription on June 2. Access continues through the end of the current billing period (July 14), and the subscription terminates on July 15.

## Cancellation Is Not a Refund

Cancelling your subscription does not automatically create a refund. Unused time in a billing period is not automatically refunded. If you believe a refund applies, review the Standard 30-Day Refund Policy or contact support. See Refund Exception Approval for cases that require approval.

## Cancellation Is Not Account Deletion

Cancelling stops future renewal; it does not delete your workspace or your data. Your workspace data is retained for 60 days after your subscription terminates. If you want to close the account, cancel first, export what you need, and then delete the account. See Account Deletion vs Subscription Cancellation.

## I Want to Cancel But Do Not See the Option

The self-serve cancellation path applies to self-service plans. If you do not see **Cancel Subscription** in **Workspace Settings → Billing**, your account may be governed by a signed Enterprise agreement. For these accounts, the customer's signed Enterprise agreement takes precedence over the standard self-service policy where the two differ. Support agents should check the account's billing arrangement before advising on cancellation.
