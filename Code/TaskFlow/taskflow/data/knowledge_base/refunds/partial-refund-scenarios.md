# Partial Refund Scenarios

## Purpose

This policy clarifies when a partial refund may be issued and which common scenarios do not create a partial refund. The default position is that most seat and plan changes affect future billing only, and partial money back is available only in the specific cases listed here.

## When a Partial Refund May Apply

A partial refund may be issued when:

- A prorated refund is explicitly permitted by an applicable policy or signed agreement, and only part of a charge is refunded.
- An exception covering a partial amount is approved under the Refund Exception Approval policy.
- A signed Enterprise agreement provides for a partial refund in a defined situation.

In each case, the partial amount is calculated using the formula in the Prorated Refund Calculation policy, unless the agreement defines its own calculation.

## Scenarios That Do Not Create a Partial Refund

- **Removing seats** — removing seats normally affects future billing only; unused time for removed seats is not automatically refunded.
- **Plan downgrade** — a downgrade takes effect at the start of the next billing cycle; unused time in the current cycle is not automatically refunded unless a policy explicitly allows it.
- **Cancellation** — cancelling does not automatically create a refund, partial or full; paid access continues until the end of the current billing period.

## Partial Amount Calculation

Where a partial refund applies, the amount is:

`Partial Refund = Eligible Unused Period ÷ Original Billing Period × Eligible Charge`

The partial refund covers only the eligible portion of the charge; the remainder of the invoice stays as billed.

## Worked Examples

### Example 1 — Downgrade does not create a partial refund

Meridian Systems holds 8 Pro seats billed monthly at $24 per user, or $192.00 per month (8 × $24). On Mar 12, 2026, the workspace downgrades to 5 Pro seats. The downgrade takes effect at the start of the next billing cycle. No partial refund is issued for the seat time not used in March, and the seat reduction affects future billing only: from the next cycle, the monthly charge is $120.00 (5 × $24).

### Example 2 — Approved partial refund on a single charge

Cobalt & Co. was charged $192.00 on invoice AF-2026-004821 for 8 Pro seats in a monthly cycle. After an approved exception, a partial refund of $96.00 was issued, equal to half of the charge. The remaining $96.00 stays on the invoice as billed. Partial refunds of this kind are case-specific and require approval; they are not a published percentage.

### Example 3 — Mid-cycle seat change contrast

Blue River Analytics added 2 Pro seats mid-cycle, generating a prorated charge on invoice AF-2026-005100. Later in the same cycle the workspace removed those 2 seats. The removal affects future billing only; no partial refund is created for the unused portion of the prorated seat charge, because the prorated charge was posted within a permitted policy and no policy in this scenario explicitly allows a refund of that unused time.

## Information Required

To assess a partial refund scenario, an agent must retrieve:

- Invoice ID and the eligible charge amount
- The original charge date and billing cycle
- The effective date of the change or refund
- The plan and seat counts before and after the change

Whether a partial refund is permitted in the customer's specific situation must be confirmed against the applicable policy or signed agreement before any amount is quoted.
