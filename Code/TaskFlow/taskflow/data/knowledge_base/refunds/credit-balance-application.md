# Credit Balance Application

## Purpose

This policy defines how credit balances on an AcmeFlow Workspace are created, applied, and eventually used or forfeited. Credits created by a refund or goodwill grant are held as a workspace credit balance.

## How Credits Are Held

- A credit created by a refund or by a goodwill grant is held as a **workspace credit balance**.
- The balance is associated with the workspace, not with an individual user or payment method.
- Credits have no cash-out value, are not transferable to another workspace, and are not exchangeable for a refund.

## How Credits Are Applied

- Credits are applied automatically to future invoices in **order of invoice generation**.
- When multiple credits exist, they are consumed against invoices in the order the invoices are generated.
- Invoice line items show the credit applied; see the Invoice Line Item Breakdown policy.

## Restrictions

- A credit balance cannot be withdrawn, transferred, or exchanged for money back.
- Credits do not change the amount of an already-posted charge.
- A credit applied to an invoice reduces the amount due on that invoice; it does not change the original charge date for refund purposes.

## Forfeiture on Cancellation

- If a subscription is cancelled, any remaining credit balance is **forfeited** unless a contract says otherwise.
- Customers who plan to cancel should be told that unused credit cannot be carried beyond the agreement's terms.

## Worked Examples

### Example 1 — Credit applied to the next invoice

Cobalt & Co. received an approved goodwill credit of $96.00 (a value equal to 4 Pro seats at the monthly rate, 4 × $24). The workspace's next monthly invoice, AF-2026-005100, is $192.00 (8 Pro seats at $24 each). The credit is applied automatically, reducing the amount due to $96.00.

### Example 2 — Credits applied across successive invoices

Northwind Consulting holds a credit balance of $120.00 from an approved refund (a value equal to 5 Pro seats at the monthly rate). Its next monthly invoice, AF-2026-005233, is $192.00. The $120.00 credit is applied, leaving $72.00 due. The following invoice, AF-2026-005401, is again $192.00; the remaining $72.00 credit is applied, leaving $120.00 due. Credits are consumed in order of invoice generation until the balance reaches zero.

### Example 3 — Forfeiture on cancellation

A workspace cancels its subscription while holding a $96.00 credit balance. Because credit balances are forfeited on cancellation unless a contract says otherwise, the remaining $96.00 is forfeited when the subscription terminates. Paid access and data retention after cancellation are covered by the Self-Serve Cancellation and Data Retention After Cancellation policies.
