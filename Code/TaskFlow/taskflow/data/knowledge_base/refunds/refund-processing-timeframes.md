# Refund Processing Timeframes

## Purpose

This policy describes how long a refunded amount typically takes to appear, by payment method. Timeframes are estimates; an agent must never guarantee an exact date.

## Timeframes by Payment Method

- **Credit and debit cards** — typically **5–10 business days** to appear, depending on the card issuer.
- **PayPal** — typically **5–7 business days**.
- **ACH** — typically **7–10 business days**.
- **Wire transfer (Enterprise)** — per agreement; may be **10–15 business days**.

These windows count in business days from the date the refund is processed by AcmeFlow, not from the date the request was submitted.

## What Affects Timing

- Card issuer processing and settlement schedules.
- Whether the original payment method is still valid and active.
- Bank or financial institution processing for ACH and wire transfers.
- Enterprise payment terms defined in the signed agreement.

## Estimates, Not Guarantees

- The timeframes above are estimates.
- A refund may appear sooner or later than the estimate depending on the financial institutions involved.
- If a refund has not appeared after the estimate window, the agent should confirm the refund status in the customer's billing record before taking further action.

## Information Required

To give a customer a timeframe, an agent must retrieve:

- The refund amount and the date the refund was processed
- The original payment method (card, PayPal, ACH, or wire)
- The invoice ID associated with the refund

## Worked Examples

### Example 1 — Card refund

A card refund is processed on Feb 3, 2026 (a Tuesday). At 5–10 business days, the amount would typically appear between Feb 9 and Feb 16, 2026, subject to the card issuer. The customer should be given this range as an estimate, not a promise.

### Example 2 — ACH refund

An ACH refund is processed on Mar 4, 2026 (a Wednesday). At 7–10 business days, the amount would typically appear between Mar 13 and Mar 18, 2026, depending on the customer's bank.

### Example 3 — PayPal refund

A PayPal refund is processed on Apr 6, 2026. At 5–7 business days, the amount would typically appear by Apr 13 to Apr 15, 2026, before any PayPal and bank-side processing.
